06 / Causal feature design

From causal hypotheses to model inputs.

The causal graph determines which signals we combine, transform and select.

STEP 01 / SELECTED PARENTS

Not every column gets equal attention.

GR · RHOB · NPHI · PEF · DTC · SP

The graph identifies these six petrophysical responses as direct parents of the target in the proposed representation. They form the starting point for causality-guided construction.

Important distinction: these are engineered functions of domain-selected variables, not experimentally proven causal effects. A separate weak-class feature augmentation and hybrid resampling strategy further address underrepresented classes.
07 / From features to predictions

Training the model. Then questioning its decisions.

Rebalance → learn → refine uncertain decisions.

STEP 01 · RESAMPLING

Reduce dominance. Support rare classes.

Under-sample majority classes; over-sample selected minority classes. Shale 30%; Sandstone and Limestone 20%; Basement ×5; Dolomite ×1.5.

Shale ↓ 30%Sandstone ↓ 20%Limestone ↓ 20%Basement ↑ ×5Dolomite ↑ ×1.5
STEP 02 · CLASSIFIER

Random Forest, trained on engineered features.

Random Forest combines decisions from many trees. Replacing it with a single Decision Tree lowered F1 by 16.16 points.

When Shale wins — but Chalk comes second.
